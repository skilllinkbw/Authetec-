🔐 Privacy Policy

How Authetec collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Privacy Policy v2.4 ✅ GDPR Compliant

1. Data Controller

Authetec (Pty) Ltd is the Data Controller for all personal data processed through the platform. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at dpo@authetec.co.bw.

2. Data We Collect

We collect: identity documents, biometric data (face images), contact information, financial details, device fingerprints, IP addresses, usage analytics, and verification results.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data is used for: identity verification, fraud detection, transaction processing, compliance reporting, customer support, platform improvement, and legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We share data with: financial institutions (for payments), verification partners (for identity checks), regulators (for compliance), and law enforcement (where legally required).

5. Data Retention

We retain data for: 7 years for transaction records, 3 years for biometric data, 2 years for analytics, and as required by law. Users may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority.

7. Security Measures

We implement: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, access controls, audit logging, breach notification procedures, and regular security assessments.

8. Cookies

We use essential, functional, and analytics cookies. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.
